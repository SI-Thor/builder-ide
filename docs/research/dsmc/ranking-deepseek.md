## 4. Where the conversations contradict or extend our design

| Our rule | What the conversation proposes | Contradicts or extends | Judgement | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reflex is fast, deterministic, never waits** | The LLM must never touch the tick; it is an asynchronous advisor whose proposals are rebased onto current state before commit. | Extends | Correct and already implied by our rule; the rebasing requirement is the new, load-bearing detail. | dsmc2.md |
| **Canon is the one history, a ledger** | The Canon is a *shared cognitive medium*, not a record — agents annotate, question, prophesy, and compete for legibility inside it. | Extends | Right direction, but "thinking as a first-class action" needs a schema before it needs a philosophy; add annotation types, not a new ontology. | dsmc2.md |
| **Mediator improves the system in motion, never on the reflex path** | The DM adjudicates gap-fillings with "yes, and" / "no, but" and accumulates precedent; the LLM proposes, a deterministic validator rules. | Extends | This is the concrete shape our mediator was missing; the validator is what keeps it off the reflex path. | dsmc2.md |
| **The room: agents connected by propagation with decay** | Stigmergy research: an always-on channel *degrades* coordination; the gate must be cost-sensitive and activated only under distress signals. | Contradicts | Our propagation-with-decay is a gate, but we have not calibrated it; the 34%/19% result says the gate is load-bearing, not cosmetic. | dsmc2.md |
| **Flow: mediator observes and steps in only when the predictive sign fades** | The human's *absence* matters more than presence; the community organizes around the return, and hospitality culture is a function of your pattern of returning. | Extends | This is the missing half of flow — we designed the mediator's stepping-in, not the room's response to being left. | dsmc2.md |
| **Anti-GAN: critics answer with a continuation, not a verdict** | The DM's rulings are verdicts (`yes_and`, `no_but`, `defer`) with rationale and precedent. | Contradicts | The DM is a *verdict engine*, which is exactly what we said critics should not be; the resolution is that the DM's verdict is a *continuation* (a filling), not a score. | dsmc2.md |
| **Three minds: reflex, canon, mediator** | A fourth role emerges: the **Biographer**, whose only job is to write to the human. | Extends | The Biographer is not a mind in our sense — it is a *voice* — but it is load-bearing enough to be a first-class role. | dsmc2.md |
| **Shared Liquid d1-2B decision model, ~64 ms per pack** | Per-agent small brains (100M–2B) differentiated by query policy and fine-tune, not by size; the shared seam is replaced by a shared *world model* or *Canon*. | Contradicts | The batched seam optimizes throughput; the conversation argues it homogenizes behavior. Keep the seam for now, but plan per-agent heads as the next step. | dsmc2.md |
| **Browser spectator view (3D and integer text)** | The Show is a *composed cut* by a Director, not a raw view; the Board is sparse and evocative, not exhaustive. | Extends | Our spectator view is a *window*; the conversation wants a *program*. The Director is the missing component. | dsmc2.md |
| **Coach is planned: cues, not commands; observes during flow** | The human is a **witness** whose attention is the rarest currency; the community reserves certain events for your presence. | Extends | This is a stronger version of the coach — the coach's *attention* is the intervention, not just the cue. | dsmc2.md |
| **LAN-only, four bots on Thor, Paper 26.1** | Server-side mods (AIBot, Numen) eliminate Mineflayer's protocol fragility at the cost of version coupling. | Extends | Worth a spike if Mineflayer stalls become the bottleneck; not worth the coupling today. | dsmc2.md |
| **Skills are cloud-written JavaScript, contextual bandit over tiles** | Skills should be verified *in imagination* before admission; the gatekeeper is a learned simulator, not live execution. | Extends | Right in principle, but the world model is a plugin; the immediate version is a *replay* gate — verify against logged decisions before live execution. | dsmc2.md |

---

**The single most important thing these conversations teach us that our design is missing:** we designed the mediator to *step in* when the predictive sign fades, but we never designed what the room does when the mediator is *gone*. The conversation's deepest move is that the human's absence — not presence — is the primary design surface: the community organizes around the return, develops a hospitality culture as a function of your rhythm, builds a model of you that you cannot see, and reserves certain events for your witnessing because your attention is the rarest currency in the system. Our three minds and our flow rule describe a system that improves in motion; they do not describe a system that *waits well*. The biographer, the five-beat return, the hospitality cultures, and the community's theory of you are not features to add later — they are the answer to the question our architecture never asked: what is the room doing while the mediator is not looking?
