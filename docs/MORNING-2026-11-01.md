## What broke, and why (each is now a rule or a queued fix)

1. **mcode's quota ran out twice:** 22:35, and again at 02:25, only ~75 minutes after a reset, with 9 seats running. **Rule: at most ~5 mcode seats at once.** Resets come about 2.5 h after the limit is hit.
2. **Dead seats leave live helpers.** A seat that dies leaves its nohup'd downloads, render batches and lock waiters running.
   - Two Seed-OSS downloaders ran at once for an hour and wrote the same chunk files. 36 chunks came out oversized; they were removed (60 GB freed).
   - **Rule:** before resuming a seat's work by hand, `pgrep -af` for its helpers.
3. **Lock starvation.** An orphaned syzygy batch held the bare `heavy.lock` for 5.4 h, re-rendering a scene its replacement had already done. The integrated night waited 3.5 h behind it. `fuser` was misleading; `/proc/locks` shows the true holder. **Rule: thor-admit leases over the bare flock.**
4. **The GPU-floor block.** For an hour, thor-admit admitted nothing while ~90 GB stood free. Its GPU floor read tegrastats' `lfb`, which on unified memory is a contiguous-block figure, not the GPU's headroom. The daemon now runs with `--gpu-floor 0`; round 10 fixes the default. During the restart, an auto-started daemon hung holding the socket lock (also in round 10).
5. **night-v3 (the first integrated night) has not completed yet.** Each run got further:
   - Run B failed in its first ledger row: `CutLine` was not JSON. Fixed by the director, `283afa7`.
   - Run C appeared to render nothing. **The director's diagnosis (a dropped `render_cycles`) was wrong.** The seat found the real cause: the engine's scratch path was relative, so run C's healthy render landed in the wrong folder (`f3e631c`, `a2ef080`). A silent no-op render is now a loud failure (`0e70b3a`).
   - Run D got through preflight (its two lines: "Tonight: 8 goals, 0.2 GPU-h of the 1.0 h bar… It fits, with 0.9 GPU-h to spare") and then failed in Infinigen: `ValueError: Proxy does not support __iter__`. The seat is on it.
